THE JOURNEY TO DANDELI

How to reach Dandeli?

Dandeli is connected by road to several cities in Karnataka and neighbouring states. Plan your route before you travel.

OPTION 01

Arrive by train

Nearby railway options include Alnavar, Londa and Dharwad. From the station, arrange a taxi or local transport to Dandeli. Check current train schedules and onward travel before departure.

OPTION 02

Arrive by road

Dandeli can be reached by road from Hubballi, Dharwad, Belagavi, Karwar and other regional cities. Travel time varies with your starting point and road conditions.

STILL HAVE QUESTIONS?
Where is Dandeli located?

Dandeli is in Uttara Kannada district of Karnataka, in the Western Ghats region. It is known for forest landscapes, the Kali River and outdoor experiences.

Is Dandeli suitable for a family vacation?

Yes. Families can enjoy nature, sightseeing and suitable outdoor activities. Choose activities according to age, health, weather and operator safety guidance.

What accommodation options are available?

Dandeli offers resorts, cottages, homestays and other accommodation types. Room facilities, occupancy and rates vary by property and date.

When is the best time to visit Dandeli?

October to March is generally popular for pleasant weather. Monsoon conditions can affect outdoor activities, especially river rafting, so check locally before travelling.

How can I enquire about packages?

Use the enquiry form or contact us through WhatsApp. Share your dates, number of guests and preferred stay type to receive current options.

Is river rafting available throughout the year?

No. Rafting depends on river flow, season, weather and local permissions. Confirm current availability with the authorised operator before making plans.
